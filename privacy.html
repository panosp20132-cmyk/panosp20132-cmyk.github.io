RevRank Uploader - Privacy Policy

RevRank Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the RevRank YouTube channel. It uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel as unlisted videos.

It only uses the permission "Manage your YouTube videos" (upload). It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of anyone. Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
